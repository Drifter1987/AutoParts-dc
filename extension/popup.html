VIN safety on a listing

A VIN panel appears automatically on supported listing pages.

Supported: Marketplace, CarGurus, Autotrader, Cars.com, Bring a Trailer, Copart.

The panel decodes the vehicle with NHTSA vPIC, counts model-year recall campaigns, and links to the VIN-specific NHTSA check and the AutoParts safety report.

Open the VIN decoder
Privacy and limits

The extension reads visible text on the current listing page to find a VIN. It sends only that VIN to NHTSA vPIC and NHTSA recalls. It does not read cookies, send analytics, or store browsing history. A detected model-year campaign is not proof of an open recall on the exact vehicle.
